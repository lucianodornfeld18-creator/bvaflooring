Our Standard

The BVA 52-Point Floor-Ready Standard

Every BVA floor passes the same 52-point Floor-Ready inspection — from subfloor moisture to the final baseboard — before we call a job done. It is how a new company earns trust the hard way: by being measurable.

📐
Site & Subfloor Assessment12 checkpoints
  1. Full walk-through & scope confirmation
  2. Existing floor & subfloor type logged
  3. Flatness check to 3/16" over 10 ft
  4. Slab crack & control-joint mapping
  5. Soft-spot & squeak probe
  6. Transition & threshold heights measured
  7. Doorway & jamb undercut plan
  8. Trim / baseboard condition noted
  9. Furniture & appliance move plan
  10. Pet & family access plan
  11. Photo documentation of starting state
  12. Written measurement & waste calc
💧
Moisture & Humidity Control10 checkpoints
  1. Digital subfloor moisture reading
  2. Calcium-chloride or RH probe on slab
  3. Ambient humidity & temp logged
  4. Vapor barrier spec confirmed
  5. Perimeter expansion gap planned
  6. Wet-area waterproofing checked
  7. AC running for stable interior RH
  8. Concrete cure age verified (new builds)
  9. Manufacturer moisture limits cross-checked
  10. Go / no-go sign-off before install
📦
Material Prep & Acclimation10 checkpoints
  1. Product matched to room & traffic
  2. Batch / dye-lot numbers verified
  3. 48–72 hr on-site acclimation
  4. Boxes opened & cross-stacked
  5. Defect & shade sort
  6. Underlayment / pad selected
  7. Layout & seam plan drawn
  8. Start wall & sightline chosen
  9. Stagger / random-length pattern set
  10. Cut-station & dust control set up
🔨
Installation Craft12 checkpoints
  1. Subfloor cleaned & fastened
  2. Self-leveler where needed
  3. Underlayment seams taped
  4. Precision jamb & casing undercuts
  5. Tight, staggered end-joints
  6. Expansion gaps held at perimeter
  7. Adhesive / lock-engagement checked row-by-row
  8. Tile back-buttered & lippage-tuned
  9. Even grout joints & full coverage
  10. Stair treads glued & mechanically set
  11. Transitions & reducers fitted
  12. Level & hollow-spot tap test
✨
Finish, Cleanup & Sign-off8 checkpoints
  1. Color-matched fill & putty
  2. Shoe molding / quarter-round reinstalled
  3. Caulk & silicone at wet edges
  4. Full vacuum & damp clean
  5. Haul-away of debris & offcuts
  6. Care & maintenance hand-off
  7. Final walk-through with you
  8. Written workmanship warranty issued
